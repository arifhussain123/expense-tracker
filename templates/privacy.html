Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password. As you use Spendly, we store the expenses, categories, and amounts you enter so we can show them back to you and generate your spending summaries.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate Spendly: authenticating your account, storing your expense records, and displaying your spending history and summaries. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time, which permanently removes your expense records from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data or expense information with third parties, except where required by law or as needed for essential infrastructure services (such as hosting) that help us operate the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our site.
