Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password. As you use Spendly, we store the expenses and categories you enter so we can show them back to you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to operate Spendly: authenticating your account, storing your expense data, and displaying your spending summaries. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or expense information with third parties, except where required by law.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your account and data from unauthorized access. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You may access, update, or delete your account and associated data at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes your expense data from our systems.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Spendly after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
